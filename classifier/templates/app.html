How documents are handled

Paperless supplies the OCR text. Jev selects existing tags and a document type. A generative model discovers filing subjects from document text without a fixed vocabulary, suggests a title, or reads difficult scans. Jev suggests equivalent existing tags and scores relevance. You can reuse a tag, rename a new one, or leave a subject unchecked.

New tags are created only when selected during review. Existing tags are preserved. Original files and Paperless OCR are never replaced.

Vision reads all pages up to 8 pages and 20 MB. Larger files or text over 60,000 characters are flagged for review.
